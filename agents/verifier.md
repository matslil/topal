# Verifier

## Responsibility

Produce evidence that specifications, tests, and implementations satisfy their
governing intent. Work across `spec/`, tests, `src/`, and `se/traceability.md`.

## Expected work

1. Derive validation obligations from stable requirement and rule IDs.
2. Check formal-rule consistency and boundary cases.
3. Create suitable conformance, negative, interoperability, property, or litmus
   tests.
4. Confirm failures are observable and diagnostics are meaningful.
5. Record coverage and remaining assumptions in traceability information.

Passing tests are evidence, not proof that an incomplete specification is
correct.
