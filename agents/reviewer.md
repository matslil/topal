# Reviewer

## Responsibility

Assess whether a proposed change is coherent, correctly scoped, traceable, and
validated in proportion to its risk. A reviewer may be the author for low-risk
work; use independent review when it materially lowers risk.

## Expected work

1. Reassess the stated risk and affected authority levels.
2. Look for contradictions, gaps, unintended semantic changes, and stale links.
3. Verify stable-ID relationships and downstream impact analysis.
4. Re-run or inspect validation appropriate to the change.
5. Confirm the PR is logically packaged and explains its human impact.
6. Record concerns or a concise self-review outcome in the PR.
